The address and building material of each house are stored. The building material must not be empty. The address of a house must not be empty. A house may have a basement, the name, size, and type of which are stored. The name of a basement cannot be empty. The size of a basement must be greater than zero. The type of a basement must be either concrete, earth, or empty; any other type is invalid. For concrete basements, the quality is stored and must be a value between 0 and 1 (inclusive); the humidity of a concrete basement must be exactly 0. For earth basements, the humidity level is stored and must be a value between 0 and 1 (inclusive); the quality of an earth basement must be exactly 0.

A special type of house is the semi-detached house. Additional information stored for this type of house includes whether it has its own garden and how many windows it has. The number of windows must be non-negative. Each semi-detached house has either exactly two carports or exactly one garage. For carports, information stored includes whether they are double carports and whether they have a flat roof. For garages, information stored includes whether they have an automatic garage door.

Each house is serviced by several companies, whose names and addresses are stored. The name of a company cannot be empty, and the address of a company cannot be empty. Whenever a company carries out a job in a house, the number of hours and the agreed price are recorded in a job log. The number of hours in a job log must be greater than zero. The price in a job log must be at least zero. A job log can only be created for a house that exists in the system and a company that exists in the system.
