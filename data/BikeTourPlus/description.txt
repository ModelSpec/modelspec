BikeTourPlus is intended to be used by local bike tour organizers to manage bike tours including participants and to book biking guides and gear for any bike tour.

The BikeTourPlus application has a built-in user account for the manager of the local bike tour organizer called manager@btp.com. Initially, the password for this account is set to manager, but this can be changed later on. The manager's password must be at least four characters long, must contain at least one lower-case character, at least one upper-case character, and at least one character from the set !#$. The password cannot be empty. Only the manager may define the start and end date of the biking season by specifying the start date and the number of biking weeks. The number of biking weeks must be greater than or equal to zero, and the start date cannot be from a previous year or earlier. Each year, these biking weeks are numbered starting with 1 and the participants are assigned their tour weeks using these numbers (i.e., either earlier or later in the biking season). The manager also specifies the weekly cost of a biking guide, which is the same for all guides; this price must be greater than or equal to zero. All of these BikeTourPlus settings (start date, number of weeks, and price of guide per week) may be updated by the manager at any time.

The manager also specifies the gear available for rent (e.g., regular bike, e-bike, helmet, etc.). Each piece of gear has a name and a price per week, where the price per week must be greater than or equal to zero, and the name must not be empty. Gear names must be unique across all pieces of gear and all combos in the system; it is not possible to add a piece of gear whose name is already used by another piece of gear or by an existing combo. The manager may add, update, or delete pieces of gear. A piece of gear that is part of an existing combo cannot be deleted. When a piece of gear is deleted, it is also removed from the gear requests of all participants who had requested it. Note that the prices of items do not have any cents.

Furthermore, the manager defines named combos, i.e., sets of commonly needed gear (at least two different kinds of gear), which can be rented with a percentage discount applied to the total price when a biking lodge is also rented. If a lodge is not rented, a combo is rented at the regular price per week (i.e., the sum of the individual prices of the gear in the combo). The combo discount must be between 0 and 100 inclusive. Combo names must not be empty and must be unique across all combos and all pieces of gear in the system; it is not possible to add a combo whose name is already used by another combo or by an existing piece of gear. The manager may add, update, or delete combos. A combo must always have at least two pieces of gear; it is not possible to remove a piece of gear from a combo if doing so would leave fewer than two pieces of gear in the combo. When a piece of gear is added to a combo that already contains that gear, the quantity of that gear in the combo is incremented by one. When a piece of gear is removed from a combo, the quantity is decremented by one, and if the quantity reaches zero, that gear is removed from the combo entirely. When a combo is deleted, it is also removed from the combo requests of all participants who had requested it.

Each biking guide registers in the system, providing their email address, password, name, and emergency contact (i.e., a phone number). The email address is used as the account name, which together with the guide's password identifies the guide in the system. Once entered, the email address cannot be changed. A guide may update their password, name, and emergency contact, but none of these fields may be empty.

When a participant registers in the system, they also provide an email address, password, name, and emergency contact. As is the case for biking guides, the email address is used as the account name and cannot be changed. The account name and password identify each participant. A participant then indicates for how many weeks they wish to go on a bike tour and their availability during the biking season (from a start week to an end week). The number of weeks requested must be greater than zero, must not exceed the total number of biking weeks in the biking season, and must not exceed the number of weeks in the participant's availability window (i.e., the available weeks from start to end inclusive). The availability window (both start week and end week) must fall within the weeks of the biking season (1 through the total number of biking weeks), and the start week must be less than or equal to the end week. In addition, a participant selects any gear and/or combos they may wish to rent (e.g., one e-bike, two bike bags, two combos) and indicates whether they want to stay at the biking lodge before and after their biking tour. A participant may update their password, name, emergency contact, number of weeks, availability window, and lodge preference at any time, subject to the same validation rules as registration; the participant's email address cannot be changed. The manager may also add or remove pieces of gear and combos from a participant's requests. When a piece of gear or combo is added to a participant who already has that item, the quantity is incremented by one; when removed, the quantity is decremented by one, and if it reaches zero, the item is removed from the participant's requests entirely.

For both guides and participants, the email address must not be empty, must not contain any spaces, and must be a valid email address (i.e., it must have exactly one @ symbol with a non-empty local part and a domain containing at least one dot, with non-empty parts on either side of the dot). The email address cannot be manager@btp.com (which is reserved for the manager). A guide's email cannot already be linked to an existing guide or participant account, and a participant's email cannot already be linked to an existing guide or participant account. The password, name, and emergency contact fields must all be non-empty for both guides and participants.

At some point before the start of the biking season, the manager initiates the creation of bike tours. The system assigns each registered participant to a bike tour, grouping participants together when their required number of weeks and availability windows allow the same contiguous block of weeks. Each bike tour is assigned one biking guide. Guides are assigned to bike tours in registration order; the first registered guide takes as many consecutive tours as possible before the next guide is used. Participants are processed in the order they registered. The start week of a bike tour is the earliest week in the availability window of the first participant assigned to that tour, and the end week is determined by the start week plus the required number of weeks minus one. Subsequent participants are added to an existing tour if their number of requested weeks and availability window are compatible with that tour's already-assigned weeks. A participant who cannot be assigned to any tour is marked as NotAssigned, and the system raises an error indicating that at least one participant could not be assigned. Successfully assigned participants are marked as Assigned.

The participant's total cost for the bike tour includes the cost of the biking guide (price per week multiplied by the number of weeks in the tour) and the cost of any gear or combos requested. For each combo, if the participant has also requested a lodge, the combo is charged at the discounted price (the sum of the individual gear prices in the combo, multiplied by the quantity, multiplied by one minus the discount percentage divided by 100, with any fractional cents rounded down); if no lodge is requested, the combo is charged at the regular price (the sum of the individual gear prices in the combo, multiplied by the quantity). Individual pieces of gear are charged at their price per week multiplied by the quantity. All of these per-week costs are then multiplied by the number of weeks in the bike tour to arrive at the total cost for bookable items. The biking lodge stay itself is provided free of charge.

After bike tours are created, the manager processes payments. To record a payment, the manager enters an authorization code for the participant; the authorization code must not be empty. A participant must be in the Assigned state to accept payment; once paid, the participant's status becomes Paid. It is not possible to pay for a participant who is in the NotAssigned, Paid, Started, Banned, Cancelled, or Finished state. Attempting to pay for a participant in the Paid, Started, or Finished state raises an error that the participant has already paid. Attempting to pay for a banned participant or a cancelled participant raises corresponding errors. Attempting to pay for a participant who has not been assigned raises an error that the participant has not been assigned to their tour.

At the start of a given biking week, the manager starts the tours for that week. For each participant whose tour begins in the specified week, the system checks their status: if the participant has paid (status Paid), their status is updated to Started; if the participant has been assigned but not yet paid (status Assigned), they are banned (status Banned). It is not possible to start a tour for a participant in the NotAssigned, Started, Banned, Cancelled, or Finished state; doing so raises an appropriate error.

The manager can view the details of a bike tour. For each tour, the view presents the tour id, start week, end week, the assigned guide's email and name, the total cost for the guide, and for each participant their email, name, total cost for bookable items, and total cost for the bike tour.

After a participant completes their tour, the manager marks them as Finished. Only participants with the Started status can be finished; their status then becomes Finished with a refund of 0 percent. It is not possible to finish a tour for a participant who has not started (i.e., is in the NotAssigned, Assigned, or Paid state), who is banned, who has cancelled, or who has already finished; each of these cases raises an appropriate error.

At the end of the biking season, all participants and guides are removed from the BikeTourPlus application. The manager may delete any guide or participant account at any time; attempting to delete an account that does not exist has no effect (if the email matches a different account type, that account is left unchanged).
